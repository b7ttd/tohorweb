Stand Locations

Our crew maintains 17 portable wooden lifeguard stands across our strip. A minimum of two lifeguards are assigned to each stand, and it can exceed to ten. Stands are subject to maintenance, closure, and other elements. At our most well-attended beaches, we place qualified EMTs, equipped with top-gear medical equipment. Our administrative office is located at Point Lookout.

Beach Wheel Chairs

Beach Wheel Chairs are avaliable to the general public, and can be retrieved at the Point Lookout office. It is a first come, first served on a daily basis. Lifeguards can bring the wheelchairs to you. Beach Wheel Chairs Hours of Operation (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM 7 days a week)
